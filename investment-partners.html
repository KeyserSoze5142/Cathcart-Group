Why Cathcart

A Partnership Built on Trust

Cathcart Group is a fully integrated Class A multifamily development, construction, and property management company. Our commitment to delivering the highest standards of quality in our relationships and in our products and services is what has allowed us to enjoy consistent success for our investors and business partners.

We have many loyal investors who embraced our long-term hold strategy in these development deals, deploying their returns from deal to deal, creating a stream of passive, cash-flowing investment assets. We firmly believe that the long-term hold strategy of our luxury apartment communities is the greatest way to generate passive income and build long-term wealth.

Investor Questions

What is Cathcart Group's investment strategy?

Cathcart Group follows a long-term hold strategy for its Class A luxury apartment communities, which we believe is the greatest way to generate passive income and build long-term wealth for our investment partners.

What does 'fully integrated' mean for investors?

Development, construction, leasing, and property management are all handled in-house by the Cathcart family of companies — aligning incentives across every phase and consistently delivering projects on schedule and within budget.

What is Cathcart Group's track record?

Since 1984, Cathcart has completed 13 developments across 4 states, owns 2,100+ units, and manages over $500 million in assets — with many loyal investors deploying returns from deal to deal.
