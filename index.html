Our Purpose

We Improve Communities and Lifestyles

Our vision: to redefine the markets in which we serve by providing superior products and positively outrageous customer service.

From its early focus on acquiring small residential properties, Cathcart made a significant leap in 1992 with the development of the 348-unit Lakeside Apartments, redefining luxury rentals in Charlottesville. Today the company owns over 2,200 luxury apartment homes with a commitment to excellence, sustainability, and community integration.

About Cathcart Group

Questions, Answered

What does Cathcart Group do?

Cathcart Group is a fully integrated multifamily real estate company founded in 1984 in Charlottesville, Virginia. It develops, builds, and manages luxury apartment communities through its three divisions: Cathcart Development, Cathcart Construction, and Cathcart Property Management.

Where does Cathcart Group operate?

Cathcart Group is headquartered in Charlottesville, Virginia, and owns and manages communities across Virginia and West Virginia, with new developments underway in Winchester and Roanoke, VA; Mobile, Alabama; and Missoula, Montana.

How many apartment units does Cathcart Group own?

Cathcart Group owns over 2,200 completed luxury apartment units, with more than 1,000 additional units in the planning stages across four states.

How do I find an apartment at a Cathcart community?

Browse the Communities section of this site to explore eight current communities. Each community page links to its own leasing website with floor plans, pricing, and tour scheduling.

How can I invest with Cathcart Group?

Cathcart Group partners with investors who embrace a long-term hold strategy in Class A multifamily development. Visit the Investment Partners page or contact the team at 434-282-2800 or info@cathcartgroup.com.
