Construction

Founded in 2007 — a hands-on builder focused on safety, quality, and the end-user experience.

Built the Cathcart Way

From Concept to Completion

Cathcart Construction provides both pre-construction and construction services, with a specific focus on multifamily construction. Leveraging our multifamily construction and development experience, we have developed a reputation for consistently delivering quality projects that exceed stakeholder expectations by providing oversight of the preconstruction and construction phases from concept to completion.

Cathcart Construction maintains a hands-on approach focusing on safety, budget, schedule, quality, code compliance, communication, and the end-user experience. We pride ourselves on our team members' years of extensive building experience and long-term relationships with suppliers and subcontractors.

Our core values, team culture, and commitment to excellence allow us to deliver high-quality properties for the Cathcart Group to own, maintain, and manage for the long-term.
