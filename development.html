Realizing Potential

Where Great Communities Begin

Cathcart Development represents a dynamic and intricate sector within our real estate services enterprise. Focused on crafting upscale communities that surpass expectations in terms of quality and lifestyle, Cathcart's development group is the driving force behind our consistently impressive returns for investors and business associates.

The pre-development stage involves an in-depth review process, where we meticulously study high-growth Metropolitan Statistical Areas (MSAs) with a demand for luxury apartment living. This evaluation encompasses demographics, household formation, absorption history, rent growth, population growth, and more. Once the target market is identified, we progress to site selection — strategically choosing raw land near emerging housing developments, major employers, grocery and retail hubs, and entertainment centers.

A Systematic Approach

In harmony with the envisioned community, we devise practical schedules and budgets that guide every phase:

  • Financing — structuring reliable capital for every project
  • Construction — delivered in-house by Cathcart Construction
  • Leasing — marketed and leased by our own award-winning teams
  • Asset Operation — managed long-term by Cathcart Property Management

This enables our family of companies to consistently deliver luxury apartments on schedule and within budget — ensuring reliable, high returns for our investors and business partners.
