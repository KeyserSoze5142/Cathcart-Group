Community Questions

Where are Cathcart Group's apartment communities located?

Cathcart Group's current communities are located across Virginia — in Charlottesville, Harrisonburg, Chester, Suffolk, and Daleville.

Are Cathcart communities pet-friendly?

Yes — Cathcart communities are pet-friendly, and many feature dedicated dog parks, dog spas, and pet amenities. Check each community's website for its specific pet policy.

How do I schedule a tour?

Each community page on this site links to the community's own leasing website, where you can view floor plans and pricing and schedule a tour.
