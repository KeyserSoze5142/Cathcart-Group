Leadership

Experienced, entrepreneurial, and family-led — the team guiding Cathcart Group into its next era.

Executive Team

The People Behind the Vision

Rip Cathcart

Chairman

Read Bio

Rip Cathcart, Chairman of Cathcart Group, with his entrepreneurial energy and dedication to quality, has created a visionary approach to the development and management of multi-use investment properties. After a successful career in real estate sales, Rip turned his keen insight toward the Charlottesville, Virginia rental market, where he ultimately developed several award-winning and highly sought-after apartment and condominium communities. From acquisition and design to construction and professional property management, Rip's forward thinking and reputation for excellence have built more than just great communities — they have enabled him to create a tremendous team at Cathcart Group that has created strong relationships between the company, its property owners, and its communities' residents.

Todd Dofflemyer

Chief Executive Officer

CPM

Read Bio

Todd Dofflemyer, the President and CEO of Cathcart Group, brought his expertise to the company in 2004 with a specific focus on exploring new development opportunities. Todd oversees the entire development process, from land acquisition to project completion, acting as a liaison between Cathcart Construction and ownership/investment groups. He has played a crucial role in overseeing the construction of over 3,000 units valued at over $750,000,000. Todd holds a bachelor's degree from Virginia Tech in Housing, Interior Design, and Resource Management with a focus in Property Management, and is a Certified Property Manager (CPM) through the Institute of Real Estate Management.

Laurin Young

Chief Financial Officer

Read Bio

Laurin Young, Chief Financial Officer for Cathcart Group, joined the company in 2009 and is responsible for all of the company's financial functions, including accounting, audit, corporate finance, IT systems management and banking relations. Laurin comes to Cathcart from Smith/Packett Med-Com, LLC, one of the largest senior housing and care development companies in the country. Laurin is a graduate of Virginia Tech with a Bachelor of Science in Accounting and Information Systems.

Steve Northcott

President, Cathcart Construction

Read Bio

Steve, as the President of Cathcart Construction, oversees the entire construction process, from preconstruction services to owner acceptance and occupancy. A Virginia Tech graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Building Construction, Steve has been actively involved in the real estate industry since 1985, with a robust portfolio spanning multifamily, single-family, healthcare, retail, and office markets across Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Florida.

Carolyn Marshall

President, Cathcart Property Management

MBA, CPM®, AMO®, RPA, AMS®, Principal Broker

Read Bio

Carolyn Marshall serves as the President of Cathcart Property Management, where she oversees a portfolio exceeding $700 million. With over 35 years in real estate, Carolyn has managed various property types, from commercial to residential. Her leadership has led Cathcart to establish a specialized division for association and investment property management, consistently surpassing leasing projections and earning industry recognition.

Donald Vogeley

Vice President, Cathcart Property Management

AMS®

Read Bio

Donald Vogeley has been a member of the Cathcart team since 1999. He is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operations of luxury apartment communities and condominium associations in Virginia and West Virginia, and serves as a central resource for operations and new business development. Prior to joining Cathcart, he was recognized with the Property Manager of the Year award at AvalonBay Communities in Richmond.

Daniel Lamay

Vice President — Development & Pre-Construction

MBA

Read Bio

Daniel Lamay joined the Cathcart Group in 2015 after a decade with regional construction management firms focused on luxury multifamily apartment homes. Dan oversees development processes (entitlements, zoning), manages project design consultants, and coordinates feedback from construction and property management to define scope and programming for each project. He is a Virginia Tech graduate with a Bachelor of Science in Building Construction and an MBA.

Carmon Jenkins

Vice President, Human Resources

Read Bio

Carmon Jenkins, Vice President of Human Resources and IT, joined the company in 2013. Her oversight includes benefit administration, employee relations, talent management, team building and strategic planning, along with managing technology vendors. Carmon graduated cum laude from Averett University with a bachelor's degree in business administration, is a member of SHRM, and brings over twenty-five years of experience to the Cathcart Group.
