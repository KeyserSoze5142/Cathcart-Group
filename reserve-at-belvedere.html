About the Community

Elevated Living in Charlottesville

The Reserve at Belvedere offers luxury apartments in Charlottesville in a variety of one-, two-, and three-bedroom floor plans. The spacious apartments include open floorplans, state-of-the-art kitchens, balconies, patios, lofts, and garages.

Resort style amenities include a saltwater pool, fitness center, dog park, car wash, car charging station, theater, game room, and more.
